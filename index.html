About

Nine years of movement, discipline, and story.

I began dancing in 2017 with MindLeaps, an organization that uses movement as a tool for emotional healing and transformation among young people in Rwanda. What started as a way to connect with others became a discipline — and then a calling.

Since then I've trained in contemporary and ballet technique, building a practice rooted in storytelling, discipline, and emotional honesty. I've performed at the Institut Français du Rwanda, the Ubumuntu Arts Festival, the Rwanda30 commemoration, and the Kigali Breaking Cypher Festival — using dance to explore mental health, identity, and healing.

In 2025 I'm developing Voices Through Movement, a new choreography on youth mental health. My goal is to keep growing as a performer while mentoring the next generation of Rwandan dancers.

"To give movement a voice — one that speaks pain, hope, and transformation."
